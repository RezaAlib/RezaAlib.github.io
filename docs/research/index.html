Research

I study user-generated content and the economics of social media platforms, using econometrics, causal inference and large-scale video, image and text data.

Papers in progress

Under review

MIS Quarterly, third-round major revision

Video ads rarely hold a single emotional tone from start to finish. This paper examines how variability in the emotions a video ad expresses over its course relates to viewership and to user engagement.

Reza Alibakhshi, Shirish C. Srivastava and Sunil Mithas

Information Systems Research, second-round major revision

A short-form video unfolds as an emotional arc that rises and falls within seconds. This paper traces how the shape of that arc relates to engagement, from first capturing a viewer’s attention to prompting them to act.

Reza Alibakhshi, Haris Krijestorac and Shirish C. Srivastava

Journal of the Association for Information Systems, under first-round review

Free social media platforms have started to give creators direct ways to earn money from their content. Using the introduction of non-fungible tokens (NFTs) as a monetization mechanism, this paper examines how the arrival of money changes content creation on a platform.

Reza Alibakhshi, Shirish C. Srivastava and Olivier Chatain

MIS Quarterly, special issue on ethics, regulation and policy, under first-round review

AI hallucinations, outputs that are fluent and confident but false, have begun to surface in legal practice. This paper examines how institutions respond to these failures and what their responses imply for governing work that is mediated by AI.

Anuragini Shirish, Reza Alibakhshi and Shirish C. Srivastava

In preparation

Journal of Management Information Systems, in preparation for submission

Followers of an influencer experience a stream of posts over time rather than one post in isolation. This paper examines how temporal variation in the emotions expressed across an influencer’s posts relates to engagement in influencer-centered communities.

Reza Alibakhshi and Shirish C. Srivastava

Working paper, data analysis

Using data from the NFT art market, this project asks whether creative work is rewarded immediately or whether its rewards arrive with a delay.

Haris Krijestorac, Reza Alibakhshi and Shirish C. Srivastava

Working paper, draft in preparation

As visual generative AI spreads, this project studies how it affects the valuation of creative work made by people.

Reza Alibakhshi and Dominik Gutt
