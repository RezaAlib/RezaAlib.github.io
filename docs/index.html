Research

My work sits where user behavior meets platform economics. One line of research measures emotion in the images, text and video that people post and asks how its dynamics relate to engagement, whether that is the gap between what a post shows and what it says, the way emotion shifts from one post to the next, or the arc a short video follows in a few seconds.

A second line studies platform decisions and their consequences for creators and audiences, such as the introduction of a stories feature or of NFTs as a way for creators to earn money. More recent projects look at generative AI and the value of human creative work, and at how institutions respond when AI systems fail.

Methodologically, I combine econometrics and causal inference on large observational datasets, often built from unstructured video, image and text, with experimental designs and computationally intensive theory building.

Background

I joined IE Business School in September 2022, after completing my PhD in Information Systems and Operations Management at HEC Paris.

Before academia I spent several years building information systems in Tehran. At Kheili Sabz Publishing Group (2015 to 2017) I worked on databases, data warehouses and business intelligence dashboards and designed the back end of a nationwide online exam system. I also worked as a consultant on enterprise architecture projects for Iran's Information Technology Organization (2016) and for Maskan Bank (2013 to 2014).

I speak Farsi and English, and French at B2 level.
