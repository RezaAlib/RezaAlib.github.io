Research

My research examines user behavior on digital platforms, user-generated content (UGC) and the economics of social media platforms. One line of work asks how the characteristics of content shape engagement. I treat the emotion in content as dynamic rather than fixed. It can diverge between the image and the text of a single post, shift across an influencer’s posts over time, and rise and fall within the few seconds of a short video.

A second line examines the strategies platforms use to stimulate content creation and consumption in online communities, such as introducing a stories feature or NFTs as a direct monetization mechanism for creators. More recent projects study how visual generative AI affects the valuation of human creative work, and how institutions respond when AI systems fail. The findings speak to practical decisions that platforms and creators face, such as when to introduce a new feature and how to structure the emotional flow of a video.

Methodologically, I combine econometrics and causal inference on large-scale observational data, much of it built from unstructured video, image and text, with experimental designs and computationally intensive theory development.

Background

I joined IE Business School in September 2022 after completing my PhD in Information Systems and Operations Management at HEC Paris.

Before academia, I spent several years building information systems in Tehran. As a business analyst and information systems specialist at Kheili Sabz Publishing Group (2015–2017), I worked on databases, ETL packages, data warehouses and business intelligence dashboards, and designed the back end of a nationwide online exam system. I also worked as a consultant on enterprise architecture projects for Iran’s Information Technology Organization (2016) and Maskan Bank (2013–2014).

I speak Farsi (native), English (fluent) and French (B2).
